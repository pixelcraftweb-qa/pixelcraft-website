COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions. Clear answers.

A few things worth knowing before we start a project.

What types of websites do you build?+

Business websites, landing pages, online store interfaces, and redesigns. The final features and integrations depend on the project scope.

Will my website work on mobile?+

Responsive layouts are part of our approach. We review the design on common phone, tablet, and desktop screen sizes.

How long does a project take?+

Timelines depend on the number of pages, content, features, and feedback. Share your requirements and we can discuss a realistic schedule.

Can I request changes to an existing website?+

Yes. We can discuss a redesign, improvements to usability, or a more complete rebuild after reviewing your current site.

Are the websites in the portfolio real client projects?+

No. They are interactive demonstration concepts created to show design and development possibilities.
