Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens when you contact PixelCraft Web through this website. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

Information you provide

When you submit our enquiry form, we receive the name, email address, selected service, message and any optional budget or timeline details you choose to provide.

Why we use it

We use this information to review your request, respond to you, and discuss a potential website project. We do not publish your enquiry as a testimonial or case study without permission.

Form provider and processing

Our contact form uses Formspree, an external service that processes submitted messages. Submissions may also be handled through our private business email and internal lead-tracking workflow. Review Formspree’s privacy information for details about its processing.

Cookies and local storage

The site does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts in this version. Your browser may retain your language choice locally. Demo store and reservation features are illustrative and do not complete real purchases or bookings.

Retention and requests

We keep enquiries only as needed for correspondence and legitimate business administration. To ask about access, correction or deletion of your enquiry, email us at pixelcraftweb.qa@gmail.com. We will review the request subject to applicable obligations.

Security and external links

We take reasonable care with submitted information, but no internet transmission is completely risk-free. Third-party services and external links have their own policies.

Changes

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The date above shows the latest version.
